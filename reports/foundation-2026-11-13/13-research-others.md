# 13 — Other apps: what is technically ahead, and what nobody does well

Research sub-report, 13 November 2026. Scope: ten apps outside the GoodNotes/Notability/Noteshelf set. Sources are vendor documentation, 2026 reviews and store listings; Reddit was unreadable this session, so sentiment comes from those pages plus `research_notes/competitors/community/POWER-USERS.md`.

## 1. App-by-app: the standout mechanism and whether our foundation can carry it

"Do we have it" is judged against this checkout, not the plan. ✓ usable; ◐ primitive exists, mechanism does not; ✗ absent.

**What this says.** These apps are not ahead on pens or PDF markup. They are ahead on **structure**: Nebo tokenises ink, MarginNote makes one atom serve many views, Flexcil/LiquidText anchor excerpts, Concepts makes strokes transformable geometry, ZoomNotes makes views objects. We are strong on ink truth, layers, export and history, and weak on every structural layer above the stroke.

## 2. Unmet power-user needs, ranked

Ranked by (frequency × severity) ÷ current coverage.

1. **Search that covers everything you wrote.** LiquidText and MarginNote OCR skip handwriting ([Paperlike](https://paperlike.com/blogs/paperlikers-insights/liquidtext-vs-marginnote)); Noteful has no handwriting search; Freenotes added it late; GoodNotes indexes it but users report resets. Transcripts, excerpts and PDF text remain separate searches. Nobody returns one hit list with a box on the page for each hit.
2. **Provenance for handwriting, not just PDFs.** Flexcil and LiquidText link excerpts to source rectangles; MarginNote Title Link works from titles only. No app lets you box a handwritten claim, jump to the page that caused it, and come back.
3. **A library you can trust and leave.** The purchase argument is trust, not features: GoodNotes export lock-in, Noteshelf `.nsa`, Notability data loss, LiquidText/MarginNote paywalled export ([POWER-USERS.md](research_notes/competitors/community/POWER-USERS.md)). No app ships a documented open bundle of ink, text, links and history another tool can read.
4. **Sustained speed on dense notebooks.** The GoodNotes 7 backlash was latency and tool-state regressions; engineering students on 0.15 pt strokes left for Noteful/Nebo. No vendor publishes a stroke, latency or battery budget, so users cannot verify what they care about most.
5. **Ink that stays editable everywhere.** Interactive Ink works only in Nebo's own formats, not arbitrary PDFs; conversion elsewhere is destructive. Users want to fix a word or restyle a line without losing the hand.
6. **A true page↔canvas hybrid.** Concepts cannot paginate or print naturally; page apps bolt on a separate whiteboard (GoodNotes, our `BoardKit`); LiquidText's workspace is another pane; ZoomNotes' sub-documents are closest but obscure.
7. **A study loop that starts from handwriting.** MarginNote builds cards from excerpts, GoodNotes generates quizzes, but turning handwriting into a mind-map node still needs manual boxing. The missing primitive is a handwriting block that is already a card candidate.
8. **Presenter mode with private cues.** Teachers on iPad → Apple TV still lack a view where cues never reach the audience: "the presenter's workspace and the audience's view are hardly ever the same thing" ([POWER-USERS.md](research_notes/competitors/community/POWER-USERS.md)). Nobody ships it well.

Lower leverage but frequent: layers/z-order non-designers can reason about; on-device AI grounded in your own ink with consent; refinement that never pauses input.

## 3. What should shape the foundation now

Ordered by cost of deferral. The first three are schema-level and unlock whole feature families.

1. **A word/line/token layer over ink — the keystone.** Per page: ordered lines, words, boxes, per-token text and confidence, derived from `PKStrokeRecognizer` but stored as our records keyed by stroke ID. Everything depends on it: search hit boxes, smart selection, undoable handwriting→text, links to a handwritten phrase, math, study cards, AI grounding, text-layer export. `SearchKit` currently stores one string per source per page — a dead end for all of it.
2. **`ContentAnchor` and `Link` as NoteCore entities.** A stable address — notebook, page, element or stroke-ID set, rect, optional text range, content hash — plus a link pointing at one. This is Flexcil's reference link, LiquidText's excerpt, MarginNote's card source and GoodNotes' internal link, expressed once. Stable IDs already exist; the anchor and link do not. Do it before sync, because anchors must survive merges and page moves. Also move `PageOutline` titles into the notebook file: a link to a title is worthless if the title does not sync.
3. **One index across ink, PDF text, typed text and transcripts.** Extend `PageTextSegment` with anchors, boxes and source kind; ingest `AudioKit.TranscriptSegment` into the same index. One query, one ranked list, one jump path. This is the clearest unmet need and the cheapest differentiator.
4. **Camera state and presentation as codable objects.** Saved view = page + rect + zoom + layer visibility; presentation = ordered views + private-cue layer. `ZoomBand` and bookmarks supply the geometry; Freeform scenes and ZoomNotes prove the model; teachers prove demand.
5. **Decide the page-in-board object now.** `BoardKit` items and `Page` are separate worlds. LiquidText's workspace and ZoomNotes' sub-documents both need "a page is an object in a container". Add the case before sync; build UI later.
6. **Make export explicit and lossless.** PDF with invisible text layer, working links, and a JSON sidecar of anchors, links and history. Vector export, content-addressed assets and snapshots already exist — assembly, not invention.
7. **Adopt a performance budget as a gate.** Stroke budget per page, first-stroke latency target, no re-index during writing, debug HUD. Speed is the top churn reason; an unmeasured foundation regresses like GoodNotes did.

**Do not build yet:** radial menus, protractors, font beautification, refinement, handwritten math, presenter UI polish. Each is cosmetic or depends on items 1–3; building first hard-codes page-level recognition.

**Stop worrying about:** pen count, brush libraries, template marketplaces, collaboration. None appear as a 2026 switching reason. `StrokeRecord` truth, layers, vector export, `ZoomBand`, split view and `StudyKit` already exceed most competitors' foundation — the gap is structure above the stroke.
