# Connected Services

## iCloud Syncing

- Enable **iCloud Syncing** to keep notes up-to-date across all devices.

  - **Last iCloud Sync** lists the date and time of your device's most recent sync.

Please note: iCloud syncing is unavailable in Notability Cloud.

## Third-Party Backup

- Enable **Third-Party** **Backup** and select a backup service.

  - Available options: Dropbox, Google Drive, OneDrive, Box, WebDAV.
