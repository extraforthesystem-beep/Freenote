# Sharing Notes

Send your notes to other apps in multiple file formats with the steps below.

# From the Library

1. Select ![](https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/qft13i0t/2624441880/5ac3bc93a65bbbc2183a98439739/menus-options-outline.png?expires=1794582000&amp;signature=c8b3eb1d66bc8fa6ce871581a93efce32f67622bcd73a9f029e90ee8bb42c7fc&amp;req=diYlEs16nIlXWfMW3Hu4gb%2BfTIb%2FztgZmlNPJsGa%2BaDIb3DINld8vDsJoMEa%0AZg%3D%3D%0A) or ![](https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/qft13i0t/2624441879/c1ce8f437946b1ee7d90067177f4/menuOptionVerticalTest-med.png?expires=1794582000&amp;signature=1bd8c4945661846ce5f322ac236b9c7a70fa0779e88cfc63fc9727f8cfba4ffe&amp;req=diYlEs16nIlYUPMW3Hu4gfv9NxAI1P3balnD2UZXJ0JLlOqqnhhitwH%2B41Gb%0AiA%3D%3D%0A) > **Select Notes**

2. Select the notes you’d like to export

3. Select **Export** from the menu that appears at the bottom:

  - **File Format:** Choose from PDF, Note, JPEG, PNG, or NTB (Notability Cloud)

    - Each file format has different options under **More options** for you to customize.

  - **Connected services**: Tap a connected service to view folders in that third-party service and export.

OR

1. Press and hold on a note.

2. Select from **Export** or **Export Options.**

  - Selecting **Export** opens Apple's share menu.

  - Selecting **Export Options** opens a menu  to choose from  PDF, Note, JPEG, PNG, or NTB (Notability Cloud). Tap on the connected service to view folders in that third-party service to export.

# From a Note

1. Open the note you’d like to export.

2. Select ![](https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/qft13i0t/2624441880/5ac3bc93a65bbbc2183a98439739/menus-options-outline.png?expires=1794582000&amp;signature=c8b3eb1d66bc8fa6ce871581a93efce32f67622bcd73a9f029e90ee8bb42c7fc&amp;req=diYlEs16nIlXWfMW3Hu4gb%2BfTIb%2FztgZmlNPJsGa%2BaDIb3DINld8vDsJoMEa%0AZg%3D%3D%0A) or ![](https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/qft13i0t/2624441879/c1ce8f437946b1ee7d90067177f4/menuOptionVerticalTest-med.png?expires=1794582000&amp;signature=1bd8c4945661846ce5f322ac236b9c7a70fa0779e88cfc63fc9727f8cfba4ffe&amp;req=diYlEs16nIlYUPMW3Hu4gfv9NxAI1P3balnD2UZXJ0JLlOqqnhhitwH%2B41Gb%0AiA%3D%3D%0A) to open the [Options menu](https://intercom.help/notability/*/articles/16300085).

  - **Share Settings** to allow others to collaborate on the note. ***Only available on Notability Cloud.***

  - **Quick Share** to export via Apple’s Share menu.

  - **Export Options** opens a menu  to choose from  PDF, Note, JPEG, PNG, or NTB (Notability Cloud). Tap on the connected service to view folders in that third-party service to export.

  - **Publish to Gallery** to share with Notability's [Gallery](https://intercom.help/notability/*/articles/16300109) community (unavailable in China Mainland, Hong Kong, and Macau).

OR

1. Tap ![](https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/qft13i0t/2624418271/53929ed33b95387024fa86858b69/share-outline.png?expires=1794582000&amp;signature=1c3b36c20cde9e3b719daa9fb179d00415acd3594c73b1b2b42e5dbb37474997&amp;req=diYlEs1%2FlYNYWPMW3Hu4gZlA79%2BNoYcpenF2TqtfCozvnFzEr5FPy3MNFgHF%0ABQ%3D%3D%0A)

2. Export via Apple’s Share menu.
