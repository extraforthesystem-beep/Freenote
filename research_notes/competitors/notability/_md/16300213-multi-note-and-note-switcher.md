# Multi-Note and Note Switcher

Multi-Note lets you open and work on two notes simultaneously in horizontal or vertical view. With the Note Switcher, you can switch between notes quickly.

[https://www.youtube.com/embed/xrE4a43-IF8?rel=0](https://www.youtube.com/embed/xrE4a43-IF8?rel=0)

# Opening Multi-Note

**iPad**

1. Open a note.

2. Swipe from the left side of the screen to open the **Note Switcher**.

3. Press and hold a note’s thumbnail.

4. Tap **Open On Right** or **Open On Bottom**.

Alternatively, drag and drop a note from the Note Switcher into the note editing area.

To change how the Note Switcher opens, go to **Settings > Note Editor > Gestures >** **Note Switcher** and select **Swipe from Left Edge** or **None**. Selecting **None** disables this gesture, and swiping from the left edge returns to the library instead.

**Mac**

1. Open a new note.

2. Right-click on a second note and select **Open in a New Window**.

# Switching Notes and Orientation (iPad only)

1. Open the **Note Switcher**.

2. Press and hold the thumbnail for one of the notes in use.

3. Select **Switch Notes** to swap the notes.

4. Select **Switch Orientation** to move between a left-right or top-bottom view.
