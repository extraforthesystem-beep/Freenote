# Notability Learn ✨

# Overview

Learn is an AI tool that makes studying simple and quick. It automatically creates summaries, quizzes, flashcards, and fill-in-the-blanks from your notes, PDFs, or recordings, letting you focus on learning with less effort.

Check out our blog post on it [here](https://blog.notability.com/post/introducing-notability-learn)!

# FAQ

**How does Notability handle my data when using the Learn feature?**
We do not use your data to train AI systems or Large Language Models (LLMs). We never access your notes unless you give us explicit permission or it's required by law, and we do not sell your data. For more details, you can view our full privacy policy [here.](https://notability.com/privacy)

**Why can't I see or use Learn?**
Learn is only available to Notability Plus and Pro subscribers. Ensure you are on the most recent version of our app if you are running into any issues.

Learn is also not available on managed devices (MDM). If you would find this feature useful, please have your IT Admin reach out to <edusupport@gingerlabs.com>

**What is the difference in Learn usage between the Plus and Pro tiers?**
Plus includes a set amount of Learn usage, shared across questions, flashcards, and other Learn features. Pro includes unlimited Learn usage.

**Why isn't a quiz being generated?**
The content must be at least 250 words but should not exceed approximately 20,000 words.

**After importing a PDF, Learn shows "No study material, yet." How can I troubleshoot this?**
The PDF file may need to be converted to a searchable format through a third-party website.

**Can I export a quiz or add it to my notes?**
Currently, exporting a quiz or adding it to a note is not available.
​
​**Can I select multiple notes to create a quiz?**
Not at this time - a quiz can only be created with one note.

**Which languages are supported?**
Learn will automatically detect the input language and generate a summary or quiz in that language.

**How can I turn off Learn?**
Navigate to Notability Settings > AI Customization > toggle Learn.
​
​**In which regions is the Learn feature available?**
This feature is available in all regions except China Mainland, Hong Kong, and Macau.
​
​**Why am I being asked to complete a survey?**
Your input about how you use Notability helps us create better community content.

**I am having issues with Learn. What should I do to troubleshoot?**

Ensure you are on the latest version of the Notability app, and update it via the App Store if you are not. Additionally, ensure you are on the most recent OS version on the device you are using. Other small things you can try to troubleshoot include closing and reopening your app, restarting your device, and making sure you have enough RAM available for optimal performance.

For further assistance, please contact our [Support Team.](https://intercom.help/notability/*/articles/16300134)
