# Image to Ink Conversion

Transform photographs and scanned documents into ink. Great for capturing classroom chalkboards and whiteboards, notes written in the physical world, and more.

- After adding an image, tap on the inserted image, tap ![](https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/qft13i0t/2624441880/5ac3bc93a65bbbc2183a98439739/menus-options-outline.png?expires=1794582000&amp;signature=c8b3eb1d66bc8fa6ce871581a93efce32f67622bcd73a9f029e90ee8bb42c7fc&amp;req=diYlEs16nIlXWfMW3Hu4gb%2BfTIb%2FztgZmlNPJsGa%2BaDIb3DINld8vDsJoMEa%0AZg%3D%3D%0A) and select **Convert to Ink**.

- After saving a scanned document, select **Insert as…Ink**.

# Supported Devices

Image to Ink is supported on all Apple Silicon devices with the Apple A12 Bionic chip or newer.
