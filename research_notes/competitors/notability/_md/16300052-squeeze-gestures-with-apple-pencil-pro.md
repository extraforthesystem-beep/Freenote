# Squeeze Gestures with Apple Pencil Pro

Squeeze gestures can be customized in your iPad settings > Apple Pencil > Squeeze. The following gestures are supported:

**Squeeze to Erase**

1. Squeeze the [Apple Pencil Pro](https://support.apple.com/en-us/120123) and hold to switch to the Eraser tool.

2. Erase as needed.

3. When you're done erasing, release to quickly switch back to the previous tool.

**Squeeze to Switch to the Last Tool Used**

*Hold squeeze (temporary):*

1. Squeeze and hold the [Apple Pencil Pro](https://support.apple.com/en-us/120123) to temporarily switch to the last tool used.

2. Use the tool while holding the squeeze.

3. Release the squeeze to return to your previous tool.

*Quick squeeze (persistent):*

1. Briefly squeeze the [Apple Pencil Pro](https://support.apple.com/en-us/120123) to switch to the last tool used.

2. Use the tool as needed.

3. To switch back, briefly squeeze again.

**Squeeze to Show Color Palette**

1. Squeeze and release the [Apple Pencil Pro](https://support.apple.com/en-us/120123) to display the color palette.

2. Select your desired color, line size, and line style.

3. The menu will automatically be dismissed after your selection.

# Arc Menu

1. Squeeze the [Apple Pencil Pro](https://support.apple.com/en-us/120123) to bring up the arc menu.

2. Draw on the note while the arc menu is open.

3. Select a tool such as a pen, pencil, highlighter, eraser, select, and undo. The color and size can also be adjusted.

# FAQ

**Why aren't the Squeeze gestures working for me?**

1. Double check that you don't currently have the ![](https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/qft13i0t/2622433254/4c3336e8857154f47cc25862c9ad/toolbox-viewOnly-outline.png?expires=1794582000&amp;signature=175d3c15df92f23dc5e0cf21ca7ee4f5feff41a0b9051b340af4ad912b15be35&amp;req=diYlFM19noNaXfMW3Hu4gRiL6K4hGNn7EKoBJmFfDlxXzUY4JMRpMap9CF28%0AYQ%3D%3D%0A) Navigate tool in your toolbox enabled. Squeeze can be triggered when using any tool **except** the Navigate tool.

2. If the above doesn't help, double check that Squeeze isn't disabled in your iPad's Pencil Settings.

**I'm having trouble holding the squeeze.**

You can adjust the sensitivity in your iPad's Pencil Settings and that should help with holding the squeeze.

**What happens when Auto-Deselect is enabled with Pro Pencil squeeze?**

Auto-deselect disables the Pro Pencil squeeze, so squeezing the pencil won't trigger its functions.
