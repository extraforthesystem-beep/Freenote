# Note Security: Locked Folders

You can create a password to lock folders within Notability.

A folder's password will automatically sync between iOS and Mac even if you don't sync notes across devices.

Password-protecting individual notes and/or the entire app is unavailable at this time.

**Please note: Locked Folders are unavailable in Notability Cloud.**

# Face ID, Touch ID, and Passcode

Notability supports your device's Face ID, Touch ID, or Passcode.

In the Library, go to ![](https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/qft13i0t/2627365718/00206fe1c6ca3785c6bb9000e332/lib-btn-settings.png?expires=1794582000&amp;signature=169b3407f5e685be7a50d7c0703fee0e1a6efa3863dcdc5e8bc15a8c7a3ab8f8&amp;req=diYlEcp4mIZeUfMW3Hu4gcxyDdTOhY32di%2F3jIscgDpVOJb99P52QXV1%2BgwZ%0A%2FQ%3D%3D%0A) > **Account** > Locked Folders, if you need to reset your password, select **Change Password**.

You can also enable your device’s Face ID, Touch ID, or Passcode to override your current password and gain access to a locked folder.

# Lock a Folder (iOS)

1. In the Library, tap and hold onto a folder.

2. In the menu that appears, click edit, then enable **Lock Folder**.

3. Select **Save**.

# Lock a Folder (Mac)

1. In the Library, right-click on a folder.

2. Click **Edit**, enable **Lock Folder**.

3. Select **Save**.
