# Search

# Global Note Search

*Search for specific notes quickly in the Library.*

1. Tap or click on the Search bar in the Library.

2. Type in a search term.

3. Results will appear on the right.

# Filtering Global Note Search Results by Folder

*Search for specific notes within a folder.*

1. Tap or click on the folder from which you would like to search notes.

2. Tap or click on the Search bar in the Library.

3. Type in a search term (Results will appear).

4. Tap or click on the Filter option located below the Search term.

5. Filtered results will appear.
