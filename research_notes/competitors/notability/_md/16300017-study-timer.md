# Study Timer

Make the most of your study time with a timer that keeps you focused and on track. Customize your sessions with built-in breaks designed to help you stay productive.

*Available on app version 16.2 or higher.*

# How to Start the Timer

1. Within a note, open the **Options** menu.

2. Tap **Start Study Session**.

3. Select your preferred study time, break duration, and set your goal for the session. Tap + to customize breaks and the total number of sessions.

4. Tap **Start Session** to begin, and the timer will appear in the bottom left corner.

# Editing, Pausing, and Editing the Timer

Tap on the timer to expand it and view study session progress.

To pause the session, tap the **Pause** button. To end the session, tap **End Session**.

To end a break early, tap on the **X** on the blue screen. Then tap on the timer and select **End Break.** The next study session will begin automatically.

Study durations cannot be modified until the current study session is complete.

# FAQ

**Where is this feature available?**
This feature is only available on iPad and Mac and is currently not available for the Web app or iPhone.

**Will my timer continue to run if I navigate to other notes?**
Yes, the timer will continue to run unless the app is force-closed.

**Can I run multiple timers at once?**
No, only one timer can be used at a time.
