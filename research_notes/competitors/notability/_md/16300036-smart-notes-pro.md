# Smart Notes \(Pro\)

# Overview

Tap record, and we'll automatically transcribe, summarize, and make Smart Notes for you in real time. Smart Notes capture and highlight key parts of your recording, while you record, so you can focus on what matters.

When you are done recording, the Smart Note will update to include details from your note's ink, text, and PDF-the final result: A fully comprehensive, easy-to-parse Smart Note for you to review.

# FAQ

**How are Smart Notes different than Live Transcripts?**
Smart Notes are structured notes created from your content, while Live Transcripts turn your spoken content into text.

**Will my Smart Notes sync across devices?**
No, but you can generate them again on your other devices.

**Can I export my Smart Notes?**
Smart Notes cannot be exported, but you can copy and paste them by using Copy All.

**What is the difference between a Smart Note (Pro) and a Summary (Plus)?**
A Smart Note is created in real time from transcripts as you record. A Summary is created after recording using your audio, handwriting, text, and PDFs.
