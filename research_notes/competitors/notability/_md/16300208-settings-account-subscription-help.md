# Account

- **Notability Account:** Log in and out of your Notability account.

  - A Notability account is used for public note sharing and [the Gallery](https://notability.com/gallery/en/discover).

    - Notes synced with iCloud aren’t backed up to your Notability account, but notes in Notability Cloud are saved to your account automatically.

  - Select the 3 horizontal dots to **Delete Account** or **Change Password**.

- **Locked Folders:** If you need to reset your password, select **Change Password**.

  - You can also enable your device’s Face ID to override your current password and gain access to a locked folder.

  - Notability supports your device's Face ID, Touch ID, or Passcode.

- **Preferred Name**: Create a name for your account and update it anytime.

# Manage Subscription

- **Current Plan**: You can see which plan you’re currently using and the date of auto-renewal.

  - **Monthly Edit Limit:** Starter users can see the percentage of monthly edits used on the progress bar.

  - **Manage Subscription:** This will take you to the App Store subscriptions page.

  - **Restore Plan**: If your plan is incorrect, tapping on this button should nudge things into order. Make sure you are logged into the same Apple ID that was used to purchase Notability and/or the Plus subscription.

  - **Refer a friend**: Send a 30-day guest pass for your Notability plan and unlock Inky for 7 days.

# AI Customization

- **AI:** Enabling this option will enable all AI-powered features.

- **Learn:** This option enables study features such as quizzes and flashcards.

- **Transcription:** Enabling this option will convert your audio recordings to text, making them readable and searchable.

- **Default Language:** Change the default language for Learn.
