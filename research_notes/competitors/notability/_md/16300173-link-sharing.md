# Link Sharing

Share links to notes which can be viewed in any browser, by anyone with a link. These notes will appear in your library under **Shared Notes**. Audio recordings are available only after someone downloads the linked note.

Link Sharing and Notability accounts are unavailable in China Mainland, Hong Kong, and Macau.

# Create a Link

1. Open a note.

2. Select ![](https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/qft13i0t/2627274388/7b76d0ac13addb8339ebe3188733/menus-options-outline.png?expires=1794582000&amp;signature=696c7872c8eb7bde8fb317cce3e177a5034da72051a8f08f5d78b0072f7997b6&amp;req=diYlEct5mYJXUfMW3Hu4gVkQQWiN9xE2Hx7%2B4Kd%2Fy7vCp3wlvhFYa5B70g7k%0AfQ%3D%3D%0A) > **Create Link**

  - You’ll be prompted to [log into your Notability account](https://intercom.help/notability/*/articles/16300212) or to create one.

3. Once the link is generated, tap **Share Link** to send the note to others.

4. If you make updates to your note after creating a link, select **Update shared link** to sync the edits over.

5. To stop sharing, select **Stop Sharing** in the same menu.

**Starter** and **Classic** users can share up to 10 notes at any given time with a maximum of 100MB per note.

Plus users can share up to 100 notes at any given time with a maximum of 100MB per note.

# Download Shared Notes

To download a note that someone has shared with you:

1. Click **Download Note** or **Download PDF** in the top right corner of a shared note.

2. Open the file (.note or .pdf) in Notability to make edits.

  - You can also open the .pdf file in other apps that support .pdf.

  - If the original note link is updated, the updates will not automatically appear in the downloaded note. You'll have to download the updated note via the shared link again.
