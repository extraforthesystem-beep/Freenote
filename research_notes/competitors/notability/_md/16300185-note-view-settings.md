# Note View Settings

View your notes in Seamless, Single Page, or Night Mode.

# Seamless

Seamless view displays the note title at the top of the page and you can scroll vertically.

# Single Page

Swipe through your notes horizontally, one page at a time. Use Single Page view with Presentation Mode, and your notes will feel like slides while presenting to others.

- Open a note and select ![](https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/qft13i0t/2629037743/bd81e3bd100a038a21a6dbe99bcb/menus-options-outline.png?expires=1794582000&amp;signature=a7e876596e5d2da7b3bb68c99a4058049b94eb1bdc3e726ff2ceae782d9ef3c6&amp;req=diYlH8l9moZbWvMW3Hu4gQwhRQWwXmQahbSNrj7wpTlasaPp0o1Wiu6YKFNj%0AFQ%3D%3D%0A) or ![](https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/qft13i0t/2629038097/95812d59409763ff4b5193a40732/menuOptionVerticalTest-med.png?expires=1794582000&amp;signature=5b5c5546fb97979367e28afda6488450d088ae86aff01f5ea20091886c032bde&amp;req=diYlH8l9lYFWXvMW3Hu4gd5SSPSXMf4TVSv80kAAksy3hLydMADa9NP7VEEn%0AwA%3D%3D%0A) > **View settings** > **Single Page**.

# Presentation Mode

Great for sharing notes in a classroom setting, during meetings, and more! Any actions that you perform in Notability (e.g. Content Manager, viewing your library) will not be displayed to the audience on screen.

Present your note in full screen, without any toolbars and menus, on compatible devices through AirPlay or when connected through an HDMI cable.

Use [Multi-Note](https://intercom.help/notability/*/articles/16300213) to view a secondary note that stays hidden from the main note while presenting. If you’d like to share your secondary note with the audience, go to Mirror and then choose Left or Right.

# Content Matches Theme

Your content will automatically match your current theme - light or dark. Learn more [here](https://intercom.help/notability/*/articles/16300151)!
