# Create and Edit Notes

# Create a Note

1. Use +**New** in the upper right corner of the Library to create a new note.

**Rename a Note**

- Open a note, and tap on the note name at the top to start typing.

- Tap and hold or right-click on a note in the Library and select **Rename**.

Renaming a note will clear and remove [previously saved iCloud versions](https://intercom.help/notability/*/articles/16300149) of that note.

# Move Notes into Folders

## **Individual Note**

1. Select and hold on a note's thumbnail.

2. Drag the note over a folder.

3. Let go to drop the note into the folder.

## **Bulk Notes**

1. Select ![](https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/qft13i0t/2624339460/5174154b506d9ff4fbeb1e9973e7/menus-options-outline.png?expires=1794582000&amp;signature=f3271549e193b13a9292bb1e63171e8a0486c285832f58d4930e0ce6223e09c9&amp;req=diYlEsp9lIVZWfMW3Hu4gaoUzqYJkwMwOyjoKwZBxHDiOA3boqtI57Km8Ys7%0ALQ%3D%3D%0A) in the upper right corner of the Library.

2. From the Options menu, pick **Select Notes**.

3. Select the notes you’d like to move.

4. Tap on the group of notes and hold.

5. Drag the notes over a folder and release to place them inside.

# Duplicate, Favorite, and Delete Notes

**Individual Note**

1. Open Notability's Library.

2. Press and hold or right-click on a note to **Duplicate, Favorite,** and/or **Delete**.

3. **Delete** by swiping from left-to-right on a note’s name.

**Bulk Notes**

1. Tap or click on ![](https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/qft13i0t/2624339910/a75dbdad235c8fda313c6df63c7e/menus-options-outline.png?expires=1794582000&amp;signature=9083a1d0ec05c0517d36381509eb9426d48ca2be1abe9783853bf8d4c32aca00&amp;req=diYlEsp9lIheWfMW3Hu4gS2Hsffq1iGFEf1q7%2BXwhRNnXnFiFnPQu73deDAl%0AuA%3D%3D%0A) in the upper right corner of the **Library** and choose **Select Notes**.

2. Pick the notes you’d like to take action with or Select All.

3. Select from **Duplicate**, **Favorite**, and/or **Delete** at the bottom menu.

# Recover Notes

Notes can be recovered for up to 30 days after you deleted them.

After 30 days, they will be permanently deleted.

1. Go to ![](https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/qft13i0t/2624340488/581d4e96389f5edfe5e8a4fffe92/lib-btn-settings.png?expires=1794582000&amp;signature=c7a0e2cf7bcf678638c78e0a78f78f505213bd899a173008a061a7003cfcb07e&amp;req=diYlEsp6nYVXUfMW3Hu4gQuIeDdH5xQ%2BdYuPM1C%2BR8B3o5V8g%2Bfufz6BP%2Bey%0A0Q%3D%3D%0A) in the upper left corner of the Library.

2. Go to the **Recently Deleted** tab.

3. Select notes and press **Recover Notes**.
