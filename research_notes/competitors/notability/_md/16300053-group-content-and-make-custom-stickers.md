# Group Content and Make Custom Stickers

Group pen or pencil content together to create your own custom stickers:

[https://www.youtube.com/embed/zRFH6LGj_Gg?rel=0](https://www.youtube.com/embed/zRFH6LGj_Gg?rel=0)

1. Tap ![](https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/qft13i0t/2624768868/9c406c698eae1c4d31cbfc37f90b/toolbox-selection-freehand-outline.png?expires=1794582000&amp;signature=fa51069bb0016723d2bed006caa6695ba3ab375e9b261cb40f600a753cc9fa62&amp;req=diYlEs54lYlZUfMW3Hu4gfvO9x6jxT2jEIqx6P8B%2BW4aRrq2H%2FAEo1y%2B8sL%2B%0ADw%3D%3D%0A) or ![](https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/qft13i0t/2624769136/25f9a1e50f85ed89b222c8201995/toolbox-selection-rectangle-outline.png?expires=1794582000&amp;signature=cd5d82986861735e92c092dc0fbc88d14803d3de9319db9df264287a2efd1938&amp;req=diYlEs54lIBcX%2FMW3Hu4gUgp8NBMYyILzsJQ2lsQZe9JytZ8mbhIThfLTPe4%0AXQ%3D%3D%0A) and draw around the separate pen or pencil content that you'd like to combine together.

2. Click/tap inside the drawn circle, and a menu will appear.

3. Select **Group**

  - You can now make changes (move, scale, rotate) to the items as one.

4. Select **Save** and go to **Stickers**. Your saved sticker(s) will appear under **My Stickers,** and you can use them in any note.
