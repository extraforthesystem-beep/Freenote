# Active Recall Studying

# Study with Tape

1. Select [Tape](https://support.gingerlabs.com/hc/en-us/articles/5554940885402) from the [toolbox](https://intercom.help/notability/*/articles/16300068) and customize the size

2. Cover terms/definitions you want to study with Tape

3. Tap to reveal and quiz yourself

4. Remove Tape with the [Eraser](https://intercom.help/notability/*/articles/16300189)

Happy studying!
