# Customize your Toolbox

Reorder, hide, or add tools to your Toolbox for quick access to the tools and styles that you need.

[https://www.youtube.com/embed/7tlZPibERW4?rel=0](https://www.youtube.com/embed/7tlZPibERW4?rel=0)

# How to Customize your Toolbox

- Swipe to the left on your toolbox and select ![](https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/qft13i0t/2624578507/9b678ff8911650b5750f8716809b/lib-btn-settings.png?expires=1794582000&amp;signature=14644cc75cd3aa8734ae54bf21a4fb0a3f1267826e087f1c4990581a27fb2a88&amp;req=diYlEsx5lYRfXvMW3Hu4gZJ6MR1cD4R70u45NTd4EmzlDJvpOauNpOFzoD7M%0AOw%3D%3D%0A).

  - Hold ![](https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/qft13i0t/2624580156/9d8e1a1f6dcba342a4585082f9ed/draghandleToolbarCustomization.png?expires=1794582000&amp;signature=2009ee53c8b70b00f7e4d0f285ba5aa92d8ae19f7c40fb692dbaf1e7e523ab71&amp;req=diYlEsx2nYBaX%2FMW3Hu4gQHDyMbLJtrDElUkB%2FZRY6%2F21Rm7lyt0ruRI8Q8f%0ALw%3D%3D%0A) and drag to reposition a tool.

  - To duplicate a tool, tap ![](https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/qft13i0t/2624580665/38639b433e26d437e9642e46882b/duplicateLibrary.png?expires=1794582000&amp;signature=c4bae1aaf112af7a15010adb229cf2fb318614ad2d65c087a081894ce8b92ca3&amp;req=diYlEsx2nYdZXPMW3Hu4gXiZyfCeiGyZfJvcMxts9m%2BE93FX6dUnAY03AKi7%0ANA%3D%3D%0A).

    - You can duplicate the [Pen](https://intercom.help/notability/*/articles/16300114), [Pencil](https://intercom.help/notability/*/articles/16300131), [Highlighter](https://intercom.help/notability/*/articles/16300200), and [Eraser.](https://intercom.help/notability/*/articles/16300189) Each duplicated tool can be set to a different custom style and/or color.

  - Tap ![](https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/qft13i0t/2624583928/4034abf729119f4ce7536ae1d717/viewToolbarCustomization.png?expires=1794582000&amp;signature=6d633f1aa834244d4a0a09eb88a1a56ad9be88654e8c21d7049acffb0f0ab3c7&amp;req=diYlEsx2nohdUfMW3Hu4gX5j%2FNH62ujp89b8uM2DHlnbTp8Pz0DETx1bHBWs%0Adw%3D%3D%0A) to hide a tool from your toolbox.

    - The tool will be moved to the bottom of the menu under **Hidden Tools**.

      - Tap ![](https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/qft13i0t/2624583299/596086db590d1822fa266d720745/hideToolbarCustomization.png?expires=1794582000&amp;signature=f18c974f72d1d4db872f2ad078c76907ca202f852e5d74708eba94227f11e6b4&amp;req=diYlEsx2noNWUPMW3Hu4gYe4zS6U%2FSE0TWp9JQGfpAYx1yTEdx35GuRLBqiU%0Apg%3D%3D%0A) next to the tool you want to add and it'll be placed back into the toolbox.

- Tap **Done** to save your changes.
