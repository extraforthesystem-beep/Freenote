# Custom Colors

Expand your color palette by saving custom colors in Notability.

Colors can be chosen with a color wheel or by entering a HEX value. The color dropper lets you select any color in your note and save it as one of your custom colors.

[https://www.youtube.com/embed/xdXoJpp20jE?rel=0](https://www.youtube.com/embed/xdXoJpp20jE?rel=0)
