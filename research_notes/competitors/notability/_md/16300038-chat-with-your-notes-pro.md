# Add an image to Chat

Ask about a photo, a screenshot, or something you drew. Send an image with a question or on its own.

**From your photos**

1. In Chat, tap the photo button next to the message field.

2. Choose a photo.

3. Ask a question then tap **Send**.

**From a note**

1. Use the lasso to select handwriting or a drawing.

2. Tap **Chat** in the menu that appears.

3. Chat opens with your selection attached.

**With drag and drop**

1. Drag and drop an image.

2. Ask a question then tap **Send**.

# FAQ

**Do I need an internet connection to use Chat?**
Yes, an internet connection is needed.

**Is Chat available on all devices?**
Chat is available on iPhone, iPad, Mac, and the Web App.

**Can I use Chat across multiple notes at once?**
Yes, you can chat across your entire library.

**Does my chat history sync across devices or platforms?**
No, Chat history does not sync across devices or platforms.

**In which regions is the Chat feature available?**
This feature is available in all regions except China Mainland, Hong Kong, and Macau.
