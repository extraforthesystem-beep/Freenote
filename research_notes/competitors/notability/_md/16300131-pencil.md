# Pencil

The **Pencil** has pressure sensitivity and texture—perfect for sketching, shading, and writing out your ideas.

# Overview

1. Select ![](https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/qft13i0t/2627320524/87e02aa5ca8bda6f8c815c5ba74d/toolbox-pencil-default-outline.png?expires=1794582000&amp;signature=aa770b537aa30340aa02439dd3d14557bdcf7d75f926667705b82dc5076bc19b&amp;req=diYlEcp8nYRdXfMW3Hu4gZAx6A29rWpyTa0HeDfvb6i%2F6ew91o3OalcfrRBi%0A6A%3D%3D%0A) in the toolbox and tap on the screen to start sketching.

2. Click or tap on the Pencil icon again to change colors and adjust pencil size.

  - Select a color from the palette to view the custom colors menu.

    - Add up to 8 custom colors for immediate access in the toolbar.

    - More colors can always be accessed through the custom colors menu.

# Shading and Pressure Sensitivity

1. Tilt your Apple Pencil sideways to shade and add dimension to your notes.

  - The more you tilt your stylus, the thicker the line is.

2. Press harder and your lines will become darker.

Please note: the Apple Pencil (USB-C) does not support pressure sensitivity.
