# Graphs

Plot a function right on your page. Type it or write it by hand, preview it live, and place it in your note. Graphs are available on all plans for iPad, Mac, and iPhone, in notes on Notability Cloud.

## Add a graph

1. Select the Text tool.

2. Tap and hold on the page.

3. Tap **+ Graph**.

4. Type your function. The preview updates as you go.

5. Place the graph on your page.

On a keyboard, press **Option + Command + Shift + M**. On Mac, you can also choose **Edit**, then choose **Insert Graph** in the menu bar. On iPadOS 26 and later, you’ll find Graph in the menu bar too.

## Convert handwriting to a graph

Converting handwriting to a graph is available on Classic, Plus, and Pro.

1. Write a function by hand, such as x - 2, y = 2x + 1, or f(x) = x².

2. With the Select tool, circle the function.

3. Tap or click inside the selection, choose **Convert**, then choose **Graph**.

## Customize your graph

- Plot up to two functions on the same axes, each in its own color.

- See points of interest: minimum and maximum for one function, intersections for two.

- Set the X and Y ranges and add axis labels.

- Use π units for trig functions.

- Turn on Graph only to hide the axes.

## FAQ

**What can’t Graphs do yet?**
Graphs don’t support pie or bar charts, more than two functions, or animated parameters.

**Why don’t I see Graphs?**
Graphs are available on iPad, Mac, and iPhone, in notes on Notability Cloud. They’re not available on web or Android yet.

**Why can’t I convert handwriting to a graph?**
Converting handwriting to a graph is available on Classic, Plus, and Pro. If you purchased Notability before subscriptions, you’ll need Handwriting Recognition or Math Conversion unlocked. On any plan, you can still add a graph with **+ Graph** and type your function.
