# **FAQ**

**Is Classic a subscription?**

No. Classic is a one-time purchase. You pay once and own it, with no recurring charges.

**Can I switch from a subscription plan to Classic?**

Yes, you can purchase Classic separately from your subscription. Note that switching doesn't automatically cancel an existing subscription, so you'll want to cancel that plan on your own if you no longer need it.

**What happens if I cancel my subscription and I own Classic?**

Your subscription stays active until the end of the billing cycle you paid for. Once it ends, you'll be moved to Classic. If you subscribe again later, you'll keep Classic too.

**Does Classic get future updates?**

Yes. You'll get all stability improvements and bug fixes, along with many new features. Features built for the subscription plans aren't included.

**Does Classic include Learn/any AI features?**

No. Learn, and all AI features are only available on Plus and Pro plans.

**I bought Notability a long time ago. How do I know if I'm a grandfathered Classic user?**

Grandfathered Classic users are those who purchased Notability using the same Apple ID prior to our switch to subscriptions. If that's you, your account was automatically upgraded to the current version of Classic. If you're not sure of your status, contact our support team and we can check your account.

Grandfathered Classic users can upgrade to Notability Cloud and get access to collaboration, cross-platform syncing, math conversion, and handwriting recognition.

**How does the purchase work for Classic users?**

Visit [notability.com/pricing](http://notability.com/pricing), scroll down to **Individual Pricing**, click the toggle on the right hand side that says **One-time**, and click **Unlock** to purchase your Classic plan.

**Is Classic available for Notability for Business users?**

At this time, Classic is only available for individual Notability users, and not business teams on a Business plan. To view our plans and pricing for business teams using Notability for Business, visit [notability.com/pricing#business](http://notability.com/pricing#business).

**What is the refund policy if I want a refund after purchasing Classic?**

If you bought Classic on notability.com, request a refund within 14 days of your most recent purchase.

If you bought Classic through an app store, that store’s refund policy applies. Request your refund through the store where you made the purchase. Google Play refunds must be requested within 48 hours of purchase.

**Why is my Classic purchase not being recognized?**

A couple of things can cause this. Make sure you're logged in with the same email you used to buy Classic. If you have an active subscription, Classic won't show up until that subscription expires.

**How can I restore Classic?**

Navigate to Notability Settings > Manage Subscription > Restore Purchases.
