# Partial Eraser

Partial Eraser allows you to precisely erase ink, and those individual ink segments can now be moved and edited.

The width and color of each partially erased segment can be edited independently as well.

**Tip**: Shapes can be drawn using the eraser tool! Select the partial eraser, draw a shape and hold down for a second until the shape autocorrects into a Perfect Shape.

# Whole Eraser

Erase entire strokes can be erased at once with the Whole Eraser.

**Tip**: If you handwrite over a shape, you can use the Whole Eraser to quickly erase the ink at once.
