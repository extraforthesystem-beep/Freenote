From inside a note, select ![](https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/qft13i0t/2624399989/4f71c4e4924a475739e9b01aff7d/menus-options-outline.png?expires=1794582000&amp;signature=d8f51eed45ffae71ac06d61d7251a6c32994cf54b8f4f39dc111ab7d462bd161&amp;req=diYlEsp3lIhXUPMW3Hu4gbIjpFX0h6s9mJ125Gyn%2BZjcLl1%2BqeM%2BbVgMKxoI%0AGg%3D%3D%0A) or ![](https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/qft13i0t/2624431671/ba67cbc6ff4dec5db0aa590416ea/menuOptionVerticalTest-med.png?expires=1794582000&amp;signature=2f33f30ae287bef5acb3da5fb296bd142b52ae68e9deb5e500ede25cd16b7a89&amp;req=diYlEs19nIdYWPMW3Hu4gVBa8j4dk5enodT8ZrMTbz%2BO43dBAnx%2FJpf2sO9C%0AGQ%3D%3D%0A) in the upper right corner to open the options menu.

In the Options menu, you can:

- **Quick Export**: Open Apple's share menu.

- **Export as:** Select a file format to export the note in, choose a connected service to export to, and access Apple's share menu.

- **Publish to Gallery:** Publish the note to the Gallery. *Not available for notes stored in Notability Cloud.*

- **Create Link:** Share links to notes that can be viewed in any browser, by anyone with the link. *Not available for notes stored in Notability Cloud.*

- **Start Study Session:** Select your preferred study time, break duration, and set your goal for the session.

- **[Template settings](https://intercom.help/notability/*/articles/16300179)**

- **[View settings](https://intercom.help/notability/*/articles/16300185)**

- Select the **note title** to view **Note Info**:

  - In the menu that appears, tap the **note title** to rename it.

  - Tap on the **folder** **name** to move the note into an existing folder or create a new folder.

  - **Last Modified**

  - **Last Synced**

  - **Created**

  - **Page Size**

  - **Handwriting Language**: To help recognize and search handwritten notes.

  - **Study Material Language:** Change the language of your study content.

  - **Word** **Count**: The number of typed text in a note, including text boxes (this doesn’t track handwriting or PDF text).

  - **iCloud Version History:** A tool to restore the previous version of the note.

  - **Note Id**

- **Version History:** To restore an earlier version of a note.

- **Notability App Settings**

- **Apple Pencil:** To write with your finger, tap ![](https://notability.intercom-attachments-1.com/i/o/qft13i0t/2599195570/5aea32871b087709616f3c0617e2/upload_8133866714862541162.png?expires=1794582000&amp;signature=d0adf927922e9ca93d7e75348f2759744e384468ac5fe3cf5e963d56f6e098e7&amp;req=diUuH8h3mIRYWfMW3Hu4gVdHt1%2Bq7PtDNX5%2FZd1V6y9%2FiYWSnxNGHwbVV%2BVc%0A0A%3D%3D%0A) to disconnect.

  - Reconnect the Apple Pencil whenever you'd like by writing on the screen with it.
