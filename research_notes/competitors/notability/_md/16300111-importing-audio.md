# Importing Audio

Audio files such as recordings and voice memos can be imported into Notability. Supported formats include MP3, MP4, and M4A.

To import from another iOS app, tap the share button in that app and select Notability.

Any content you add to a note while playing back imported audio will sync with the recording.

# **Transcription**

Transcription is available for recordings created in iOS apps (such as Voice Memos), as well as MP3, MP4, and M4A files.
