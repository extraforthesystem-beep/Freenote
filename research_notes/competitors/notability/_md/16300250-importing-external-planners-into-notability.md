# Importing External Planners into Notability

Download planners online and import them into Notability to use as templates!

[https://www.youtube.com/embed/irlxOdSqIFQ?rel=0](https://www.youtube.com/embed/irlxOdSqIFQ?rel=0)

1. Tap +**New** in the Library.

  - Alternatively: Tap and hold +**New** and select **Import**.

2. At the bottom of the note’s page, select **Import**.

3. Tap on the file you’d like to import into this note.

  - Alternatively: To import files from other apps, use the share feature in those apps.

4. While in a note, open the [Content Manager](https://intercom.help/notability/*/articles/16300226).

5. On the desired page thumbnail, tap or click the ![](https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/qft13i0t/2624441880/5ac3bc93a65bbbc2183a98439739/menus-options-outline.png?expires=1794582000&amp;signature=c8b3eb1d66bc8fa6ce871581a93efce32f67622bcd73a9f029e90ee8bb42c7fc&amp;req=diYlEs16nIlXWfMW3Hu4gb%2BfTIb%2FztgZmlNPJsGa%2BaDIb3DINld8vDsJoMEa%0AZg%3D%3D%0A) icon > **Create Template**.

6. Name the template and **Save**.

  - This will be saved in **My Templates** under **Custom Templates**.
