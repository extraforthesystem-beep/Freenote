## Viewing 2026 at a Glance

- Tap Year to see an overview of the entire year 2026.

## Add Events to Google Calendar from your Planner

- When viewing a specific day in your planner, click on a time slot. This will automatically open Google Calendar with that date and time pre-filled, making it easy to add an appointment, reminder, or event directly from your planner.

## Navigating Months, Weeks, and Days

- To go to a **Month** use the tabs on the right side.

- To view a specific **Week** tap on the week number on the left of the calendar.

- Once you're in the monthly overview or weekly view, tap on any **Day** to see its details.

## Index Page

- Tap 1x on the Index Page to see the 2026 Calendar and the Table of Contents.

- Tap 2x to open Finance Overview.

## How to use the Template Page

1. Navigate to the Template Page.

2. Copy or duplicate a template.

3. Paste below the desired page.

4. Tap 2x to open the Routines.
​

    Please Note: Templates cannot be pasted directly on top of an existing page.

Tips:

- Always copy the original template before adding notes to keep it unchanged.

- To add notes on a hyperlinked page, take a screenshot and paste it as an image. This way, you can write over it without changing the original template.

## Yearly and Monthly Pages

- **Yearly Pages**: Tap 1x on "2026" tab to view yearly page. Tap 2x for Yearly page 2.

- **Monthly Overview**: Double-tap a monthly tab to access the Monthly Overview.

- **One Line a Day**: Triple-tap a monthly tab to access this quick daily note feature.

## Notes Index and Hidden Panels

- To access the **Notes Index 50 Subjects**, tap the Notes tab in the bottom right corner. Double-tap to open a blank notes page.

- **Hidden Panels** (numbered 1-12) are located at the bottom and serve as blank spaces you can customize to fit your needs.

## Calendar Integration

- To access Google Calendar and/or Apple Calendar, tap on the tabs in the bottom-left corner of the planner.

- To link to Apple Calendar, please install the [Shortcut app](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/shortcuts/id1462947752)first and then download the shortcut created by Nozomu Noto [here](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1QHYEEbZqdJ9qQAvTuh0lQTRhuYzR6Xhg/view?usp=drive_link).

- In Safari, tap on Downloads and tap on Nozomunoto shortcut. Once in Shortcut app, tap Add Shortcut

- Once the shortcut is added, you can tap on A on the bottom tab and your shortcut prompt will appear.

## Additional Writing Space and Adding Media

- Choose the Folded Magazine style for extra space to write and draw.

- Personalize your pages by adding [Media](https://intercom.help/notability/*/articles/16300120) to them.

## Tutorial Page

- To access the Tutorial Page, tap 2x on the tab in the bottom left-hand corner.
