# Add and Edit Media

Use the **Add Media** tool to take photos, scan documents, and add images to your note. Enrich your note with GIFs, stickers, and image playground. Insert sticky notes for quick reminders.

Media can be placed and moved around anywhere on the note.

## Image Playground

Bring your ideas to life by using your photo library, text, and creativity to design styled images.

1. Tap or click ![](https://notability.intercom-attachments-1.com/i/o/qft13i0t/2599198039/9d2a137235bfa8a93f57c7c14946/upload_1112643358273112467.png?expires=1794582000&amp;signature=e399900d0a0087bdfcdc4cd9c22bb0f9ac37b910219027997d157d36ff6acf83&amp;req=diUuH8h3lYFcUPMW3Hu4gW7ATzFpwDhUrV8EfpfCHg%2FqbPU49tCPcIwdvGcS%0AUg%3D%3D%0A), then select ![](https://notability.intercom-attachments-1.com/i/o/qft13i0t/2599198057/68fc87b8ae3eff9d79ba539bb29c/upload_6504273099526013666.png?expires=1794582000&amp;signature=bbebead1414627c8dc1af772ed7e77f199a02322fb22b1823e38535c15faf65d&amp;req=diUuH8h3lYFaXvMW3Hu4gYB95oxlKN9s%2F%2FWWzOQRIcuqv5S1%2B80DnM4F5Ved%0Aqg%3D%3D%0A)

      Image Playground from the menu that appears.

2. Create an image by:

  - **Tapping on a concept**: Choose from suggestions, themes, expressions, costumes, accessories, or places.

  - **Using a description**: Tap on "Describe an Image" and type your description.

  - **Using an image of a person:** Create an image based on someone from your library.

  - **Using any photo:** Upload or take a photo.

3. To adjust the **Image Style,** tap **Style** and choose from Animation or Illustration.

  - To explore different versions of an image, tap the preview bubble and swipe left.

4. Tap **Done** in the top-right corner to save the image to your note.

*Image Playground is an Apple Intelligence feature, so it's only available on devices that support Apple Intelligence.

**You'll need:**

- An Apple Intelligence-compatible iPhone, iPad, or Mac

- iOS 18.2, iPadOS 18.2, or macOS 15.2 or later

- Apple Intelligence turned on in Settings (or System Settings) > Apple Intelligence & Siri

- At least 7 GB of free storage

- Your device language and Siri language set to the same supported language

Image Playground supports fewer languages than Apple Intelligence does overall, so it may not be available even if other Apple Intelligence features are working on your device.

Apple updates its compatibility list with each new release. For the current list of devices, languages, and regions, see Apple's [Apple Intelligence availability page](https://support.apple.com/en-us/121115).

If you don't see **Apple Intelligence & Siri** in your settings, your device doesn't support it.
