# Creating and Styling Perfect Shapes

Draw a shape, then hold the stroke until the ink snaps into place. Shapes can be styled and manipulated like regular handwriting.

You can create perfect circles, ellipses, triangles, squares, rectangles, pentagons, hexagons, and arrows.

# Enable Perfect Shapes

- In the Library, go to ![](https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/qft13i0t/2624342837/7cfd4835cc6c8a4b69e001f0656d/lib-btn-settings.png?expires=1794582000&amp;signature=9b5abff1b8053078ed5042e19e009ea9e46649a92232d9d47e1181b73ddf9a65&amp;req=diYlEsp6n4lcXvMW3Hu4gddSp%2BdmPXbRIpW29fhUndQQJ8%2FvjrJbSHd%2Fu7wr%0A6w%3D%3D%0A) > Note Editor > Handwriting & Drawing > Shapes Detection.

- Tap to select a shape, using any tool except the hand tool. Tap the shape with the Apple Pencil or one finger; two fingers if not using the Apple Pencil.

  - To expand or minimize a shape, tap and hold while creating it.

  - The red lines are snapping guidelines to help keep shapes aligned with other shapes and the grid paper.

# Stroke and Fill Color

- Tap to select the shape, then tap on the style menu to select **Stroke** and **Fill**.

# Fill shapes with Ink Fill

*Only available on Notability Cloud.*

**On iOS:**

1. Select the Pen, Pencil, or Highlighter.

2. Select any color.

3. Press and hold inside a closed shape you’ve drawn.

4. To change the color, select a new one and press and hold inside the shape again.

**On Mac:**

1. Select the Pen, Pencil, or Highlighter

2. Select any color.

3. Click and hold inside a closed shape until it fills.

4. To change the color, select a new one and click and hold inside the shape again.

To disable Ink Fill navigate to Notability Settings > Note Editor > Handwriting & drawing > Ink Fill.

# Locked Shapes (Only available with Notability Cloud)

- Draw a shape.

- Tap to select the shape.

- On the menu that appears, tap **Lock**.

- To unlock, select the shape and tap **Unlock.**

# Tips

**Rectangles and ovals** can be resized proportionally using two fingers.

To change a **triangle**’s shape, tap on a point.

Choose a point on a **straight line** to move it.

Draw **curved lines** with three points for adjustments. This can also snap back into a straight line.

Draw **perfect arrows**, starting with the line first.

Shapes can also be drawn using the **eraser**. Select the partial eraser, draw a shape and hold down for a second until the shape autocorrects into a Perfect Shape.
