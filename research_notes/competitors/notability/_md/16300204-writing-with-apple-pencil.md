# Writing with Apple Pencil

Notability is fine-tuned for and works best with an Apple Pencil (gen 1 & 2).

**Highly Responsive Ink:** Notability tracks the Apple Pencil's position as closely as possible to make writing fast, fluid, and lag-free.

**Pressure-sensitivity:** To make lines thicker, thinner, or anything in between, simply apply more pressure to create wider lines.

*Please note: the Apple Pencil (USB-C) does not support pressure sensitivity.*

**Dashed & Dotted Lines:** In addition to normal strokes, dashed and dotted lines can be used.

**Mirror Ink (iOS only):** Flip ink horizontally or vertically.

Using Mirror Ink:

1. Select ink.

2. From the overflow menu, select Mirror horizontally or Mirror vertically.

**Apple Pencil Double-Tap Option:** Customize your second-generation Apple Pencil with the following steps in your device’s **Settings > Apple Pencil > Actions**:

1. Switch between Current Tool and Eraser

2. Switch between Current Tool and Last Used

3. Show Color Palette

4. Show Ink Attributes

5. Turn Off

**Auto-Deselect Eraser:** Automatically switches back to the last used tool after erasing. This can be disabled in **App Settings > Note Editor > Handwriting & Drawing > Auto-deselect Eraser.**

*Please note: multiple erase marks while squeezing are not possible.*

**Palm Detection:** Notability tells the difference between an Apple Pencil and your palm, so your notes stay free of unwanted marks while resting your hand on your iPad's screen.

**Haptic Feedback:** Feel a gentle vibration when drawing a shape or moving content around.

Haptics can be disabled in **Notability Settings > Note Editor > Handwriting and Drawing > Apple Pencil Haptics.**

*Please note: Haptic feedback is only supported on Notability Cloud on an iPad Pro with Apple Pencil Pro, running iOS 18 or later.*

**Hover:** Shows a preview of the area the pencil will select.

Hover can be disabled in **Notability Settings > Note Editor > Handwriting and Drawing > Apple Pencil Hover.**
