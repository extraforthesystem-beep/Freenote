# Overview

You can search for handwriting across all of your notes or within a specific note.

Notability can only process the handwriting in your existing notes while the app is open.

1. Open a note.

2. Tap on the Select icon in the toolbox.

3. Circle the ink you want to convert and tap inside the box.

4. Select **Convert**

5. Select **Text** and your handwriting will instantly be converted into text.

6. Select **Math** and handwritten equations will be converted into hi-res, scalable images.

# Editing Ink

1. Tap on the Select icon in the toolbox.

2. Circle the ink you'd like to edit.

  - Tip: Transform an existing line into a dotted or dashed line by going to **Style** and then selecting a **dotted** or **dashed** line style.

3. To **move** handwriting, tap/click and drag it.

4. To **style, cut, copy, or delete** handwriting, tap inside the dotted line.

5. To **paste** handwriting, tap and hold at a new location after using cut or copy.

6. To **resize** handwriting, place two fingers in the selected area and pinch or expand.

7. To **rotate** handwriting, place two fingers in the selected area and rotate.

# Grouping Ink and Making Stickers

[https://www.youtube.com/embed/zRFH6LGj_Gg?rel=0](https://www.youtube.com/embed/zRFH6LGj_Gg?rel=0)

1. Tap the Select icon and draw around the separate ink that you'd like to combine together.

2. Click/tap inside the drawn circle and a menu will appear.

3. Select **Group**.

  - You can now make changes (move, scale, rotate) to the items as one.

4. Select **Save** and go to **Stickers**. Your saved sticker(s) will appear under **My Stickers** and you can use them in any note.

# Conversion Language

Notability uses your device's language. If your iPad is set to English, for example, Notability will use English in your notes.

**Supported Languages**

English, Danish, Dutch, Filipino (Tagalog), French, German, Indonesian, Italian, Japanese, Korean, Malay, Norwegian Bokmål, Polish, Portuguese, Russian, Simplified Chinese, Spanish, Swedish, Thai, Traditional Chinese, Turkish, Ukrainian, Vietnamese

# Math Conversion

[https://www.youtube.com/embed/TVZtGXqBmW8?rel=0](https://www.youtube.com/embed/TVZtGXqBmW8?rel=0)

Color-coded equations will retain their original colors.

Math Conversion supports common mathematical expressions, elements, and rules including:

- Latin alphabet

- Digits

- Mathematical symbols and operations

- Greek symbols

- Mathematical terms: sin, cos, tan, mean, median, mod, norm, ceil, cons, sort, sad, var, etc.

- Chemical elements

- International convention units (weight, length, frequency, luminosity, dosage, pressure, etc.)

- Rules: horizontal pair, fence, square root, fraction, subscript, vertical pair, matrix, partial fractions, and more.

[https://www.youtube.com/embed/Z6EdJnAFRa8?rel=0](https://www.youtube.com/embed/Z6EdJnAFRa8?rel=0)

**LaTeX**

After converting handwriting to text, you can edit the Math Equations by modifying the underlying LaTeX.

- Right-click or tap anywhere on your converted equations to access LaTeX editor options.

- When editing an equation, you are free to add in any LaTeX you'd like.
