# All Notes, Recents, Favorites, and Shared

Select from the following:

![notes categories.png](https://notability.intercom-attachments-1.com/i/o/qft13i0t/2599200607/3f1d15b1b91456107849fe37e2a9/upload_4420507005766883665.png?expires=1794495600&amp;signature=6ac288fc11f1083e602c927568b3e4334820c93281f1531723ec054e65821548&amp;req=diUuH8t%2BnYdfXvMW1HO4zRL%2BrTys8vlIcMPg3vWlBwiNfILv8dL%2BIr24jHQp%0Ab4sL03RkAr8L9ISvs1E%3D%0A)

- **All Notes**: Every note in your Library

- **Recents**: The last ten notes you opened

- **Favorites**: Notes that you’ve favorited for quick access

  - To favorite a note:

    - Press and hold (iOS) or right-click (Mac) on a note’s thumbnail in the library and select **Favorite**.

    - Select ![](https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/qft13i0t/2624441880/5ac3bc93a65bbbc2183a98439739/menus-options-outline.png?expires=1794582000&amp;signature=c8b3eb1d66bc8fa6ce871581a93efce32f67622bcd73a9f029e90ee8bb42c7fc&amp;req=diYlEs16nIlXWfMW3Hu4gb%2BfTIb%2FztgZmlNPJsGa%2BaDIb3DINld8vDsJoMEa%0AZg%3D%3D%0A) or ![](https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/qft13i0t/2624441879/c1ce8f437946b1ee7d90067177f4/menuOptionVerticalTest-med.png?expires=1794582000&amp;signature=1bd8c4945661846ce5f322ac236b9c7a70fa0779e88cfc63fc9727f8cfba4ffe&amp;req=diYlEs16nIlYUPMW3Hu4gfv9NxAI1P3balnD2UZXJ0JLlOqqnhhitwH%2B41Gb%0AiA%3D%3D%0A), go to **Select Notes**, select the notes you want to favorite, and tap or click on the **Favorite** button.

- **Unfiled Notes**: Notes that have not been saved within a folder

- **Shared** **Notes**: Notes have been shared via [Link Sharing](https://intercom.help/notability/*/articles/16300173)
