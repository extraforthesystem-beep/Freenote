# Tape

Learn from your notes with **Tape**!

Cover study content (e.g. terms, definitions) with Tape and quiz yourself. Tap to reveal the hidden areas, and check to see if you had the right answer.

[https://www.youtube.com/embed/hbpEilKZYx4?rel=0](https://www.youtube.com/embed/hbpEilKZYx4?rel=0)

## **Add Tape**

1. In the toolbox select ![](https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/qft13i0t/2717222334/35b110566d424947888bcf21d1ba/tapeToolbox-outline.png?expires=1794582000&amp;signature=8d2324a3ae4e9eb1dc44f88f91f24dfc0f7f796f84a05cba475ee9e660878713&amp;req=dicmEct8n4JcXfMW3Hu4gUwoH6VAkBfEDq2Fmp8y2OQNiQD2qqP4wBqeEjHX%0A%2Bw%3D%3D%0A).

  - Choose from 12 size steps.

2. Draw, as you typically would, over any note content that you’d like to hide and Tape will cover it.

  - When drawing vertically, hold to make a straight line first and then draw.

3. Tap on tape to reveal the content.

  - A colored border will appear around the revealed area. Tap inside the outline to re-cover with tape.

## **Change Tape Color**

1. In the toolbox select ![](https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/qft13i0t/2717222334/35b110566d424947888bcf21d1ba/tapeToolbox-outline.png?expires=1794582000&amp;signature=8d2324a3ae4e9eb1dc44f88f91f24dfc0f7f796f84a05cba475ee9e660878713&amp;req=dicmEct8n4JcXfMW3Hu4gUwoH6VAkBfEDq2Fmp8y2OQNiQD2qqP4wBqeEjHX%0A%2Bw%3D%3D%0A).

2. Choose one of the four colors or create a custom color. A total of eight custom colors can be saved in the toolbar. Tapping any color allows up to 64 custom colors to be added to the palette.

  - To  delete a color, tap and hold the color, then tap **Delete**.

## **Remove Tape**

- Erase with Full Eraser to remove Tape from the area completely.

## **Hide and Show All Tape**

- Draw over any note content as you normally would.

- Tap the ![](https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/qft13i0t/2717225414/fbc8eccb2dd1e5b4419977edd7df/hideToolbarCustomization.png?expires=1794582000&amp;signature=9d14fbe543301ff69e349b2433b685a7334d3d8b2ca7b96bd13d842ec4c20e6f&amp;req=dicmEct8mIVeXfMW3Hu4gXksc8llm7hPUsJDJeeEL2pIuX5tLU0PYk96wwiO%0AGg%3D%3D%0A) to hide all tape. ## **Sharing Notes with Tape**

When exporting a note, Tape will not appear in the exported version.

**Exception**: If the note is uploaded to the Gallery with tape on it, then the tape will appear and work for anyone that downloads it from the Gallery in .note format.
