# Recording and Playing Audio

Audio recordings are linked to your annotations. While playing the audio, you can tap anything in a note to jump to that point in the recording. Annotations you add while playing a recording are also synced to the recording.

# Playing Audio

- Press the **Play** button to enter playback mode.

- Use the **Hand** tool and tap a word, sketch, or photo to hear what was said at that exact moment.

- In the second menu that appears, select and drag the circle to scroll through a recording.

- Drag the circle back to slow down the speed as it scrolls left and right.

- Select ![](https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/qft13i0t/2627295278/24fd3fae90c0f379d36e40186c90/toolbox-10secsforward-outline.png?expires=1794582000&amp;signature=dd7ef61e9204378feb3dcba82727ccb03bda401c7d0888820c94a6f13d2b860d&amp;req=diYlEct3mINYUfMW3Hu4gQriJGMLkOumBMSe%2FZhi3FmxBpQVH7IrgOW%2FWEN5%0Azw%3D%3D%0A)![](https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/qft13i0t/2627295759/7bd6b6dc43e2f2ea63bf5089398d/toolbox-10secsback-outline.png?expires=1794582000&amp;signature=51bf4372a7099a1643116dcd7cf2612aae33559fe8e7688d2f6ddd6ca212c692&amp;req=diYlEct3mIZaUPMW3Hu4gdUxD%2Ba5wMN3kCXaS7TRddXATSRzYD%2F6sJAuMSLy%0AJg%3D%3D%0A) to rewind or fast forward 10 seconds.

# Editing Audio Recordings

- Select ![](https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/qft13i0t/2627294516/bf5f4420ae64d8eec76e6ed7cf4c/menus-options-outline.png?expires=1794582000&amp;signature=ae76d5a04c84c18a4e8c9a64410a0ff79d1bf9990a199ccf00c210ed15ec16a7&amp;req=diYlEct3mYReX%2FMW3Hu4gZ5SoTSMDi7IZptD8I1gTtGVfNrEH%2BhOZwRXrA%2BI%0ARA%3D%3D%0A) to open your list of recordings > **Edit**.

- Tap on a recording > **Edit Clip** to trim or split a file.

  - The black bars can be dragged to trim the recording.

  - Tap **Split** to split the recording and then drag the bars to edit each portion.

  - From this menu, tap on the recording name to rename it. Tap **Done** to finalize those changes.

- To split a recording into multiple sections, name the first piece and use the second piece to make an additional edit.

- Tap **Select** to take action with specific audio files.

  - Tap **Merge** to combine audio recordings into one file.

    - Imported audio can not be merged at this time.

- Tap **Delete** to remove the audio recordings from the note.

- To **Rename** recordings select ![](https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/qft13i0t/2627294516/bf5f4420ae64d8eec76e6ed7cf4c/menus-options-outline.png?expires=1794582000&amp;signature=ae76d5a04c84c18a4e8c9a64410a0ff79d1bf9990a199ccf00c210ed15ec16a7&amp;req=diYlEct3mYReX%2FMW3Hu4gZ5SoTSMDi7IZptD8I1gTtGVfNrEH%2BhOZwRXrA%2BI%0ARA%3D%3D%0A) to open your list of recordings > **Edit**.

- Tap on a recording > **Edit Clip** > rename recording. ***Available on Notability Cloud only.***

# Importing Audio

Audio files, such as MP4s and voice memos, can be imported into Notability.

Adding content to the note while playing back the imported audio will sync that content with the recording.

If the audio is in an iOS app, tap the share button and send it to Notability.

# Exporting Audio

When sharing a note, there are two ways to export the note's audio:

1. Under **Format**, select **PDF** and enable **Recordings** toggle. This shares your note as a .zip file that includes your recordings.

2. Under **Format**, tap **Note**. This shares your note as a Notability file that includes your recordings.
