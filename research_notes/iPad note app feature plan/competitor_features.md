## Q1. Complete current feature list of Goodnotes (Goodnotes 6 and any successor)

### Takeaway
Goodnotes in Nov 2026 is a three-format app (Notebook / Whiteboard / Text Document) with pen, tape, elements, layers (early access), Smart Ink handwriting editing, Restyle handwriting beautification, AI across notes/text/math/meetings, audio recording with transcription, study sets, PDF annotation, real-time collaboration and a Marketplace — sold on a five-tier ladder (Free / Special Edition one-time / Essential / Pro / Teams+Enterprise) with AI metered by monthly "AI credits". ## Q3. Document model and organisation model — how Goodnotes and Notability actually differ

### Takeaway
Goodnotes is a **multi-format** app: fixed-page notebooks, an infinite whiteboard, and reflowing text documents, organised in **nested folders** with custom colours and covers. Notability is a **single-format** app: page-based notes that can be *viewed* as continuous vertical scroll (Seamless) or page-by-page (Single Page), organised in **nested folders** (after Subjects + Dividers were retired in March 2026). So the two have converged on folders but diverged on formats: Goodnotes has three canvases, Notability has one canvas with three view modes.

### Cited Findings
- Goodnotes' three surfaces are named explicitly as Notetaking, Whiteboard ("Collaborate visually with infinite canvas") and Text Document — https://www.goodnotes.com/
- Goodnotes' plan row "Whiteboard" is a top-level file type counted toward the Free limit ("Up to 3 notebooks, 3 text documents, and 3 whiteboards") — https://support.goodnotes.com/hc/en-us/articles/13808767840015-Compare-Goodnotes-Plans-and-Features
- Goodnotes Free caps **folders at 3** while paid tiers are unlimited, implying folders are a first-class, nestable container — same URL
- Goodnotes folder colours were expanded from 9 presets to full colour wheel/HEX/RGB, described as making "your library easier to scan at a glance" — https://www.goodnotes.com/blog/whats-new-in-goodnotes-july-september-2026
- Goodnotes Layers operate **per page** ("the Layers panel on the page"), inside Notebooks only, iPad/Mac, Pro — https://www.goodnotes.com/blog/whats-new-in-goodnotes-july-september-2026
- Notability: Seamless view = vertical scroll with the note title at top; Single Page = horizontal page swiping that doubles as slide-like presentation — https://support.gingerlabs.com/en-us/articles/16300185-note-view-settings
- Notability's default view mode for new notes is a global setting (restored in v16.5.2, "Brought back the global setting to choose your default view mode for new notes") — https://support.gingerlabs.com/en-us/articles/16300220-app-update-history
- Notability replaced Subjects + Dividers with **folders** in March 2026, explicitly "providing deeper note organization with less complexity", then added nested-folder browsing from the note list in Aug 2026 — https://support.gingerlabs.com/en-us/articles/16300220-app-update-history
- Notability's organisation also carries a note-level **cover** and a per-note "sort to subject" legacy action; covers arrived v16.9 — https://support.gingerlabs.com/en-us/articles/16300220-app-update-history
- Goodnotes "Workspaces" (separate environments per client/team, switch without logging out) is dated **Coming 2026** — a container *above* folders — https://www.goodnotes.com/

### Inferences
- "Continuous scroll" is a **view**, not a data model, in Notability: pages remain discrete and reorderable in a Page Manager, so a long lecture is still N pages. Goodnotes' whiteboard is the opposite: one unbounded plane with no page concept.
- For a new app, choosing page-based-with-optional-continuous-view (Notability) is cheaper to sync and print than true infinite canvas, but infinite canvas is where whiteboard collaboration, mind-mapping and Freeform-style thinking live.
- The Subjects→Folders migration is strong evidence that **two-level subject/dividers taxonomies lose to unbounded nesting** in this market; Notability spent a release removing its own hierarchy.
- Layer support interacts badly with the folder/infinite-canvas split: Goodnotes limits Layers to notebooks (not whiteboards) and to iPad/Mac, which is the cheapest possible v1.

### Gaps
- Goodnotes' maximum folder nesting depth and whether tags/smart folders exist (beyond Education "Class folders") are unconfirmed.
- Whether Goodnotes notebooks support **page insert/delete/reorder with reflow** or fixed page numbering, and whether page sizes can differ within one notebook, is unconfirmed.
- Notability's per-note page size behaviour ("Note size matches the dimensions of an imported PDF", v14.9, 2024) suggests per-note sizing; whether mixed page sizes are allowed inside one note in 2026 is unconfirmed.

## Q4. Current pricing and what sits behind the paywall

### Takeaway
Both apps are now **freemium with a hard file-count wall and a metered AI tier above it**. Goodnotes: Free (3 files) → Essential **$11.99/yr** → Pro **$35.99/yr** → AI Pass **+$9.99/mo**, plus a regional one-time "Special Edition" on Apple only and Teams at **$120/seat/yr**. Notability: Starter (5 notes) → Lite **$11.99/yr** → Plus **$15.99/yr** → Pro **$79.99/yr**, plus a revived one-time "Classic" and Business at **$60/user/yr**. Goodnotes is markedly cheaper at the top end; Notability charges ~6.7× Goodnotes' Pro price for its unlimited-AI tier.

### Cited Findings

**Goodnotes (observed 12 Nov 2026, https://www.goodnotes.com/pricing)**
- Free **$0** "Free forever": "Up to 3 files, 5MB import size", note-taking tools, "20 mins of audio recording"
- Essential **$11.99/yearly** (7-day trial): unlimited files, unlimited audio recording, all note-taking tools, "Access limited AI capabilities"
- Pro **$35.99/yearly** (7-day trial, "Most popular"): everything in Essential + Drive integration, private link sharing, real-time collaborations, sync across devices, AI capabilities
- AI Pass **+$9.99/month** on Essential or Pro
- Teams **$120 per seat/yearly** (30-day trial): SAML SSO, domain control, consolidated billing, users & admins management, advanced collaborations, M365 Outlook calendar integration

## Q5. What changed in 2025–2026 and where the products are heading

### Takeaway
2025–2026 was an **AI-and-plumbing** period, not an ink period. Notability rebuilt its nervous system (Notability Cloud + web app, Aug 2025; folders replacing subjects, Mar 2026; Live Collaboration and Business, Mar 2026; Classic one-time, Jul 2026) and layered a study engine on top. Goodnotes shipped handwriting *editing* (Smart Ink → Restyle), Layers after years of requests, meeting AI, and a credit-metered AI add-on, while pushing cross-platform parity and enterprise/education packaging.

### Cited Findings

**Goodnotes timeline**
- Sep 2025: plan structure replaced — "Goodnotes All Platforms Yearly" became **Essential**; the Goodnotes 6 one-time payment became **Special Edition**; the Android & Windows Yearly plan is being **retired** — https://support.goodnotes.com/hc/en-us/articles/13808767840015-Compare-Goodnotes-Plans-and-Features

- Goodnotes is optimising for **professional/enterprise documents** (contracts, meetings, PDFs, AEC/legal/government verticals); Notability is optimising for **students and lecture capture**. That split is the clearest strategic signal in the 2026 material.

## Q6. The alternatives — niche, standout, pricing; and table stakes vs differentiators

### Inferences
- A credible 2027 iPad-first app needs, at minimum: pressure ink + highlighter + eraser modes + lasso + shapes, templates/covers, nested folders, PDF annotate, handwriting search, handwriting→text, typed text boxes, PDF/image export, iCloud sync, presentation mode. Everything else is positioning.
- The three genuine moats observed are (a) **audio-ink time alignment**, (b) **layers**, and (c) **handwriting editing that preserves the writer's own hand**. All three are data-model problems, not UI problems — they must be designed into the stroke format from day one, not added later.
- Free tiers are being used as acquisition, not product: 3 files / 5 notes / "3 journals, 3 pages" are all sized to force a decision within one semester.
- The macOS companion is where the whole field is weakest: Notability's handwriting recognition and audio sync are iOS/Android-only, MyScript's Pencil-free Mac experience is described as inferior, and Goodnotes gates Layers and presentation mode by platform. A Mac app that can *search and edit* handwriting well (not just view) is an open flank.
